Discover the amazing creatures living under the sea!

Explore Sea Animals

Dolphin

Dolphins are intelligent marine mammals. They communicate using clicks and whistles.

Sea Turtle

Sea turtles are reptiles that live in the ocean. They have strong flippers for swimming.

Shark

Sharks are powerful fish with sharp teeth. They play an important role in ocean ecosystems.

Octopus

Octopuses have eight arms and are known for their intelligence and ability to camouflage.

Starfish

Starfish are sea animals that usually have five arms and live on the ocean floor.

Jellyfish

Jellyfish have soft, transparent bodies and move through the water with gentle pulses.
